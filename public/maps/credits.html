Geographic data credits

Each district download contains editable outlines and street lines in local metre coordinates. We projected, clipped, rounded and simplified the geographic data for level-ground exploration. Street widths and building heights include estimates.

OpenStreetMap layers in the downloads are © OpenStreetMap contributors and are available under the Open Database License 1.0. OpenStreetMap credits.

Philadelphia

Building footprints from the City of Philadelphia are subject to the City GIS terms. Streets, paths and parks are from OpenStreetMap. Center City data.

City Hall uses the municipal building outline and courtyards. Its tower and ornament are architectural interpretations. The stone texture derives from Marble005 by ambientCG, CC0 1.0, with JPEG conversion and adjusted roughness.

Hong Kong

Buildings, streets, green spaces and the Victoria Harbour shoreline are from OpenStreetMap. Mong Kok, Yau Ma Tei and Tsim Sha Tsui data.

Tokyo

Buildings, streets and green spaces are from OpenStreetMap. Shibuya, Akihabara Chuo-dori, Kabukicho and Ueno Ameyoko data.

Montréal

Buildings, streets, paths and green spaces are from OpenStreetMap. Old Montréal data.

Melbourne

© City of Melbourne, CC BY 4.0. The 2018 Building Footprints and Road Segments with Surface Type provide the building outlines, carriageways and nature strips. Central business district data.

Helsinki

Building outlines and green surfaces are from the City of Helsinki building register and YLRE public-area layers. © City of Helsinki, CC BY 4.0. Streets and paths are from OpenStreetMap. City geospatial data. Central and south Helsinki data.
